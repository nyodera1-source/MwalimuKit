## Summary ↔ body conflicts

1. **Sub-strand count:** Summary lists 9 sub-strands; body has 27 (9 per strand × 3 strands). The summary's 9 are the distinct skill types, not the theme-specific instances.
2. **Numbering:** Summary uses 1.3 twice and 2.2 twice; body uses sequential 1.1–1.9, 2.1–2.9, 3.1–3.9.
3. **Lesson allocation:** Summary shows strand-level totals (27/18/9); body shows no per-sub-strand counts. The strand totals are NOT assigned to sub-strands in the module.

## What the previous draft got wrong

- Listed only 9 sub-strands (from the summary) instead of 27 (from the body).
- Used paraphrased outcomes (e.g., "use vocabulary on informal greetings appropriately") instead of the published outcomes.
- Assigned the strand total (27) to sub-strand 1.1.
- Used the summary's duplicate numbering (1.3 twice, 2.2 twice).

## Unresolved items

- The summary's duplicate numbering (1.3, 2.2) cannot be reconciled with the body's sequential numbering. The body is the authority.
- The strand-level lesson totals (27/18/9) are not per-sub-strand; the module leaves per-sub-strand `suggestedLessons` unset.
